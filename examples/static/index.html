anchor

A click glides the page to a section, the top or the bottom — the same glide with or without Lenis.

The header's menu and the side list follow along: the link to the section you are in is marked. The ↑ button shows after a screen of scrolling and slides out of the way of the dark band in section Two and of the footer.

One

Reached from a <div>: data-pg-anchor="#one" makes it clickable, reachable with Tab and pressed with Enter. Every section lands below the fixed header — scroll-padding-top on the page, plain CSS, a different height on phones.

Two

Scroll slowly past the dark band below: the ↑ button slides up out of its way, keeping --pg-anchor-avoid-space, and back down once the band has gone by — data-pg-anchor-avoid on the band. The footer is avoided the same way.

The "3rem lower" button lands here 3rem further down than the menu does: --pg-anchor-edge on that button. A length, so it can change per screen size.

data-pg-anchor-avoid — the ↑ button slides above this band, then back down.

Three

That link has data-pg-anchor-hash="true": the address now ends in #three, and Back returns to where you were. Without it the address is left as it is.

Four

data-pg-anchor-duration="400" on that button; the rest take the 1200ms default. Turn the wheel during a glide and it stops — as with Lenis.

Five

Scroll on to the footer: it is data-pg-anchor-avoid too, and the ↑ button steps above it. A section too close to the end to climb to the line under the header is still marked once the page has reached the bottom.

A footer, avoided too.
